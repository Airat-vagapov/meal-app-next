Creamy lemon pasta

25 min Easy 92% match

Overview

Bright, creamy and ready in 25 minutes. This lemon pasta is built for busy weeknights without tasting like a compromise.

Serves 2 Vegetarian 4.8 from 342 cooks

Ingredients

Method

  1. Cook pasta in salted boiling water until al dente. Reserve ½ cup pasta water, then drain.

  2. Warm olive oil in a large pan. Add garlic and cook for 30 seconds until fragrant.

  3. Add lemon zest, lemon juice and cream. Stir gently to combine.

  4. Add pasta and parmesan. Toss, adding pasta water until glossy.

  5. Season, finish with parsley and serve immediately.
